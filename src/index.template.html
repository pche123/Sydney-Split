What is Sydney / Split?

An experimental boundary-discovery atlas using actual ABS 2021 SEIFA data and ASGS SA1/SA2 boundaries. It generates many candidate composite signals, computes a nested contiguity-constrained Ward hierarchy, draws 2–7 colour-coded territories, follows real shared region borders, and ranks for coherent rather than scattered divides.

Important limits: a top-ranked split is not a statistically significant discovery or an explanation of individual behaviour. Several ABS indicators are correlated or derived from each other. The city view currently includes 301 selected, bounded urban SA2s. Current OSM businesses, if loaded, are temporally mismatched to 2021 Census data and may be incompletely mapped. Brands are availability patterns, not resident personality tests.

To use cultural signals, click Load live OSM (requires internet and an available public Overpass server) or download the prepared Overpass query, run it in Overpass Turbo, export as JSON, and import it here. Without a real POI payload, no Red Rooster or Latte geography is claimed.

Strength is a transparent exploration score based on variance explained across territories, border contrast, region-size balance and geographic simplicity; this is a non-inferential ranking. Results are automatically de-duplicated. Names are user-generated labels and saved locally in this browser. For publication, use spatial null controls, held-out areas, repeated spatial scales, and time-aligned external data.

Sources: Australian Bureau of Statistics, 2021 SEIFA SA1 & SA2 Tables; ASGS Edition 3 GDA2020 SA1 & SA2 shape boundaries; optional live OpenStreetMap/Overpass under ODbL. Generated with local scripts, no simulated observations.
